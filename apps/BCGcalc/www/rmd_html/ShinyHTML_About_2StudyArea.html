The tools were calibrated for small to mid-order freshwater wadeable streams in Oregon and Washington. The MaritimeNW BCG and BioDiversity Index were calibrated for the Pacific Maritime Region (western ecoregions only, minus the Klamath). The rest provide statewide coverage.
